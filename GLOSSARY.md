# TEDx committee coordination

The language of negotiated committee work and its accountable delivery.

## Language

**Synergy agreement**:
A versioned negotiation between a requesting committee and a receiving committee about one contribution to a parent deliverable.
_Avoid_: Committee copy, automatic assignment

**Agreement revision**:
An immutable set of proposed output, acceptance criteria, effort and deadline, confirmed by the relevant committee heads. A receiving head's named-owner confirmation completes the agreed terms.

**Contribution**:
One committee's agreed output toward a deliverable, with one named owner and one shared delivery task. Completing its task does not itself accept the parent deliverable.

**Counterproposal**:
A receiving committee's proposed revision requiring the requesting committee's explicit confirmation before activation.

**Safe proposal preview**:
The intentionally shared negotiation terms available to current participating heads without granting access to the private parent deliverable or its files.
