## Scope and definitions

`read_reports(term,week,unit,filters)` requires current core workspace.read and reports.view. Operational rows additionally require current committee.manage for the work's lead unit and can_access_work(detail). Only unarchived ordinary tasks/deliverables enter aggregates or source links. Restricted work, raw requests, finance, evaluations, profiles, bodies and attachments are excluded, including when a head separately has a private record grant. Reporting authority never grants source access. A unit outside the caller's current scope is denied; all supervised units form a union without duplicate work.

The report states its server refresh date/time and Manila timezone. Workload and pending queues are current snapshots, not a reconstructed historical week. The selected Monday–Sunday period applies to activity and completed response/decision samples. Closed work-list filters are all, overdue, blocked and unassigned; unsupported keys/types reject. They affect the work source list; summaries keep the whole permitted unit scope. At most50 work rows use UUID-keyset paging. Queue/handoff investigations show the oldest20 and state the full permitted denominator, with inbox links for additional records. Authorization and scope repeat on every call. Canonical title/reference source links retain the term. No export endpoint or per-member ranking exists.

| Measurement | Definition |

| Overdue now | Open work past its current timestamp, or past its untimed Manila due date. |

## Weekly activity and collection gate

Three new app_private tables hold immutable policy versions, version-bound trusted human/test/bot classifications, and minimal events. They force RLS and deny raw access to anon/authenticated/service roles. No public policy or classification editor/provisioning command is provided. Actual privacy basis, eligible population, retention approval and institutional authority must be supplied and reviewed before trusted provisioning. The migration and live synthetic preview contain no enabled policy. Provision a reviewed policy/classification set in one approved transaction; policy/revocation concurrency needs provider rehearsal before live activation.

The latest approved policy defines a new observation window. Earlier policy events are not silently replayed into a new policy's metric. Core roster rows in currently active approved states must have a trusted classification; unknown eligible coverage makes activity unavailable. Eligible humans overlap the elapsed part of the week through joined_at/ends_at, counted once regardless of units. Scheduled future joins neither inflate the current denominator nor disable coverage before they become eligible. This uses currently approved roster/account states and recorded membership intervals, not a reconstructed historical roster census. Events must fall inside both membership and capture intervals. Midweek pre-join events and events at the next Monday boundary are excluded. The numerator counts distinct eligible humans with an allowed event in Monday00:00 through the following Monday00:00, Asia/Manila, end exclusive. Partial capture/current weeks are labelled.

The target uses exact integer arithmetic, active×5≥eligible×4. Required active members are ceil(0.8×eligible): at89 eligible,71 fails and72 passes regardless of display rounding. A zero denominator has no percentage or target verdict. Cohorts below5 disclose no exact counts; this engineering floor is not an institutional privacy/anonymity approval. Unit activity breakdowns are closed to prevent subgroup differencing. Unknown configuration, expired retention, no eligible cohort and suppression are distinct states. Nothing infers newcomer success, affiliation comparisons or sustained four-week adoption from activity.

The fixed event vocabulary is work_viewed, task_updated, synergy_proposed/responded, handoff_submitted/accepted, request_submitted, decision_recorded, onboarding_item_completed, config_published and archive_verified. Named business-audit mappings, the fixed work-opening RPC and the existing authorized onboarding completion command can insert. Real existing task/request/review/synergy/handoff commands and P35 publication are integration-tested; P38's actual `publish_process_change` audit key is mapped and has a trigger regression. Published resource versions also map to config_published; private draft saves do not. `archive_verified` remains reserved until a verified archive command is implemented in its selected future step; no archive success is fabricated.

Own onboarding progress captures only a newly completed item, under the existing term lock/current authority/version/exact-key checks. Skip, re-mark and exact retry do not create new completion events or replay old operations into a replacement policy. Its object reference is an opaque term/item/revision digest, without profile/progress text or a name. A late event failure rolls back progress and its retry receipt. Internal capture helpers are private/raw-denied, never arbitrary client ping APIs.

`record_work_view(work_id)` rechecks a current permitted task/deliverable detail and derives actor, term, unit and server time. The frontend invokes it only after a visible, explicitly opened record is allowed, once per mounted page. Background scope/detail reads, focus polling, login and report refresh do not emit work views. The backend deduplicates the actor/work/Manila-week view; tests/bots/guests/unclassified actors do not record. Permission is checked before duplicate handling. No service actor may call this API. Client delivery is best effort; there is no offline queue or guaranteed attention measurement.

Action events derive from redacted authoritative audit insertions in the same transaction. An exact business retry adds no duplicate event, and a late outbox failure rolls back the event and business action together. Events contain only UUID, server timestamp, term, policy-salted pseudonymous subject key, fixed event type, opaque object/unit and deduplication key. No member name/email, comment/request body, amount, contact, token, session replay, external tracker or arbitrary client event payload is collected.

Approved raw-event retention is bounded7–90days. Service-only prune_usage_events(term) deletes at most5,000 expired event rows per call and retains current rows. No schedule is installed or activated. Periods outside the current approved observation/retention window are unavailable rather than fake zero-use weeks. Historical aggregate retention, classifier/policy lifecycle, holds/disposal, actual consent/privacy decisions and operational cleanup custody remain P46/provider inputs; policy/classification history is not automatically erased with events.

## Queue, turnaround and workload definitions

General-service queue metadata requires both current committee.manage on the receiving unit and existing independent workflow_manager authority (current department.review and head deployment, excluding self-review). Typed request storage spines are always restricted: the current and frozen revision's ordinary payload classification, general governed kind and existing metadata entitlement determine this projection. Restricted payloads, FIN/MCD cases and archived work are excluded before counts. Until current assignment grants package access, the report uses the safe type label rather than the submitted title. It never returns payloads, notes, reviewer/member identities, manifests or files. A report role never supplies review/package access.

Queue age is refresh minus current revision submitted_at, including pauses. Reassignment does not reset it. Current paused/in_review cases are partitioned into missing current reviewer assignment, other entry prerequisites, and assigned review. Approved, rejected, cancelled and requester-revision states are outside this pending-review denominator. Missing/future source timing stays unavailable.

Review turnaround counts reviewer decisions (approve/reject/request changes), joined to the exact immutable assignment/revision/instance/stage/generation. Samples are decisions recorded in the selected week; elapsed days run from assignment to decision. Current source authority and ordinary frozen payload classification apply even to historical samples. This is per-decision elapsed time, not whole-case resolution time or a FIN/MCD reliability verdict.

Handoffs additionally require current participant visibility and detail access to the ordinary source and any successor, plus a currently supervised source/receiving committee. A head who lacks participant/source access gets no count or link. First response runs from the submitted revision to the first accept/decline/request-changes by that revision's receiver; sender cancellation is excluded. Acceptance runs to the immutable receipt for that same receiver/revision. Resubmission has a new revision; exact retries cannot inflate samples. Historical revision samples require the currently visible receiver/unit. Pending count/age is a current snapshot; sample inclusion uses response/receipt time in the selected week.

Median and p90 use continuous percentiles of elapsed calendar days, rounded for display; sample counts are explicit. No samples means null/unavailable, never a zero-day performance claim. These are not business-day SLAs: agreed exam/holiday pauses and business-hour calendars need institutional inputs. No calendar or threshold is invented.

Assigned workload uses the same ordinary-task/detail/supervised-lead scope as work reports: open assigned/unassigned, due today through the next six Manila dates, open-now tasks due in the selected week, and undated tasks. It is a current snapshot. Restricted tasks/contributions remain excluded; their agreed effort stays in authorized detail pages. Any estimate projected within the ordinary scope is an explicit current contribution revision band, never inferred hours/productivity. Missing estimates remain unrecorded, not zero effort. No individual breakdown/export exists.
